## Source mix of the 33 primaries

Parts by kind: cue 35, design_note 3, donor 39, native_borrow 1, native_keep 33, sheet 54. New donor cells by game: Emerald 35, Ranger: Shadows of Almia 10, Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky 3, Diamond/Pearl 1. No donor style was preferred: each part was taken from whichever pool showed the most legible, role-correct art in the rendered boards. Emerald supplies most *small named sprites* (its sprites are named and were the most legible stills), while Ranger 2 / PMD Sky / Diamond supply the larger shaded or spiral art where nothing else fit. If you want a different balance, the alternatives swap sources without changing the choreography.


Primaries add **1453** new OBJ tiles in total across 33 moves (4 add none); 3 are custom composites (Double Team, Hypnosis, Swords Dance).
